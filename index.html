OpenClaw App

OpenClaw App is a private integration with a single user: its owner. It connects the owner's own Google account to a personal assistant that runs on the owner's own server. It is not a public service and nobody else can sign up for it.

What it does

  • Reads the owner's Gmail inbox to tell him when a message needs his attention, and saves drafts that he sends himself.
  • Reads the owner's Google Calendar to remind him of upcoming appointments.
  • Reads and writes files in the owner's Google Drive when he asks for it.

See the privacy policy for how this data is handled.
