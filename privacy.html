Privacy policy for OpenClaw App

Last updated: 11 October 2026

OpenClaw App is used by one person only, its owner, with the owner's own Google account. It has no other users and collects no data about anyone else.

Data the app accesses

With the owner's consent the app accesses the owner's Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Drive through the Google APIs.

How the data is used

The data is used only to carry out what the owner asks for: notifying him about mail that needs attention, preparing drafts, reminding him of appointments, and reading or writing files in his Drive. To do this, content may be processed by AI model providers that the owner has chosen. It is not used for advertising, not sold, and not passed to anyone else.

Storage

The app runs on a server operated by the owner. Access tokens are stored there in encrypted form. The owner can revoke the app's access at any time in his Google Account under "Third-party apps and services".
